Where these definitions come from

PG-13 and R are worded from the Motion Picture Association's own rating definitions. The MPA's R says outright: “May contain nudity, including graphic nudity” — so an R tier that only permitted revealing clubwear was stricter than a real R rating, and has been widened. PG-13's own definition says sexual activity “does not involve nudity”.

XXX is not an MPA rating. X was one until 26 September 1990, when NC-17 replaced it — X had never been trademarked, so the adult industry adopted X and XXX as self-applied labels and the MPA lost control of the mark. NC-17 is the real top rating and is considerably tamer than this tier. The XXX wording here is the owner's own and is deliberately not an MPA definition.
