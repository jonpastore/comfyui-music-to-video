Write the arc

One request over every track on the album, in order, with its lyrics. The storyboard writer for a song is then told its own beat, the close of the track before it and the open of the track after.

Story only. The arc never carries content rules. xAI is the default for an adult album; a sanitised arc writes every storyboard against a story with the teeth taken out.
