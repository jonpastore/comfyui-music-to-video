Write the arc

One request over every track on the album, in order, with its lyrics — because what track seven does depends on what track six did. It decides what the record is ABOUT, what each song does inside that, how each one opens and closes, and where a fade to black earns its place. The storyboard writer for a song is then told its own beat, the close of the track before it and the open of the track after, which is what makes scene one of track four follow scene twelve of track three.

Story only. The arc never carries content rules. The tier's wording is composed at render time by guardrail.build_prompt(), and that stays the one place content is decided — what you type here and what the model writes back are both screened before either is stored.
