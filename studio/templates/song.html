Replace a span — cut from the middle

The one thing that really does cut from the middle: ffmpeg removes the span, ACE-Step writes a bridge to fill it, and the seams are crossfaded so the join does not click. The bridge is generated slightly longer than the gap because {{ "%.2f"|format(2 * 0.25) }}s of it is eaten by the two crossfades, so the track comes back the length it went in.
